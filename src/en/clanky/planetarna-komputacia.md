The seminar starts from Benjamin Bratton's idea that computation is no longer a set of tools but a layer that transforms landscapes, energy and the sovereignty of states.

It connects Bratton's programme The Terraforming at the Strelka Institute with the comparative planetology of the Slovak philosopher Lukáš Likavčan, the work of the design studio Metahaven and Bruno Latour's thinking about the Earth. It asks what it means to design at a time when sensors, data and models determine how we know the planet at all.
