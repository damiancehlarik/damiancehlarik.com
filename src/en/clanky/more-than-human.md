Design does not only design things. It designs who has a voice. The seminar asks how political thinking would change if non-human beings and technologies entered it, and whether that would help respond to environmental crises.

The seminar has three modules. Cartographies of Presence trace how the figure of the “human” came into being and who was left outside it (Latour, Yusoff, Danowski and Viveiros de Castro). More-than-Human Coexistence asks who counts as a participant in the world (Povinelli, Haraway, Tsing, Oliveros). Desirable Futures try to design without the human at the centre (MacCormack, Barad, Butler).

The structure draws on the Postnatural Independent Program and the More-than-Human anthology. The seminar ends at home, with Slovak and Central European art from Sikora to Oto Hudec, and with a workshop: students write a book of grievances on behalf of one non-human inhabitant of Bratislava, such as the Danube or a tree on a housing estate.
