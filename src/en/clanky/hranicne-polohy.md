The seminar works with extreme situations that show design is never neutral.

It moves through five blocks: Hack, Control, Exclusion and Punishment, Destruction and Embodiment. Its starting point is Foucault's panopticon as a machine that separates seeing from being seen. The seminar then turns to texts on necropolitics, colonialism and resistance (Mbembe, Fanon, Wolfe, Simpson, Coulthard). It closes by returning to the question of what it means to design when design is always also an instrument of power.
