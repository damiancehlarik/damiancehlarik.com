A city is usually discussed through urbanism: through buildings, plans and traffic. Landscape of the City looks at Bratislava from the other side, through the landscape it stands on.

The city is made of three landscapes: the forest of the Little Carpathians, the water of the Danube and the vineyards at the foot of the hills. Each carries its own economy, materials and rules. The project follows on from the doctoral research on the normative forces of design: where that research traced how norms designed the flat, Landscape of the City extends the question of the designed environment beyond it, into the city and the region. It is being developed on the Society platform.
