Damián Cehlárik teaches the course for the Department of Visual Communication at AFAD. It aims at depth rather than breadth: each lecture follows one discipline from its beginnings, through its key elements, to the works a student should know.

The lectures move discipline by discipline, from Identity and Type onwards. Each lasts an hour and is followed by a half-hour workshop. The opening slide always shows a historical picture, an engraving or a drawing, as a reminder that the discipline is older than it seems.
