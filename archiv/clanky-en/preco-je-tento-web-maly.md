Full-resolution photographs, animations, tracking scripts. A typical personal portfolio takes longer to load than to read.

![Placeholder image](/img/zastupny-web.png)

This site is built differently. Images are 1-bit, dithered into dots and tinted with a single colour. Every page shows its own weight in the footer. The site has no database, does not track visitors and runs no scripts. It is just text and images.

The mechanics are borrowed from the solar-powered website of Low←Tech Magazine. The tone and the structure of the articles come from the website of the studio Formafantasma.
